Safety first

Never ride a bicycle with faulty brakes or another safety-critical problem. Complex repairs should be handled by a professional bicycle mechanic. If shared equipment is broken or missing, report it before use.

Step-by-step

Simple maintenance guides.

These guides cover basic checks only. Stop if you are unsure.

01
◉

Inflate your tyres

Remove the valve cap and attach the pump securely. Pump in short bursts, then remove the pump and replace the cap.

  • Check the valve type first.
  • Use the correct pump head.
  • Follow the pressure range on the tyre.
02
◌

Check tyre pressure

Tyres should not look flat or have visible damage.

  • Look for cuts, bulges and embedded objects.
  • Use a gauge when available.
  • Check pressure before longer trips.
03
⌕

Identify a puncture

A tyre that keeps losing air may have a puncture or a damaged valve.

  • Inspect the tyre carefully.
  • Do not handle sharp objects with bare hands.
  • Ask a mechanic if you are unsure.
04
✓

Basic bicycle checks

Make a quick check before riding to catch common problems.

  • Check tyres and wheels.
  • Look for loose parts.
  • Make sure lights and reflectors are present where needed.
05
↔

Check your brakes

Brakes should feel firm and stop the bike safely.

  • Squeeze both brake levers.
  • Walk the bike and test each brake gently.
  • Do not ride if either brake is faulty.
06
⚒

Use tools safely

Use the right tool for the job. Never force a bolt or adjustment.

  • Keep fingers away from moving parts.
  • Return tools after use.
  • Report broken or missing equipment.

About the project

A shared space for
better everyday cycling.

The Neighbourhood Bike Hub brings shared bicycles, basic repair equipment and practical information closer to residents in Gotland.

How the hub works

Housing providers and housing associations install and manage a physical Bike Hub within residential properties. Registered residents can borrow normal bicycles, electric bicycles and cargo bicycles for free, or use the repair station for their own bike.

The website helps residents check availability, request a booking, read repair guides and report issues. The housing provider manages the fleet, maintenance, resident access and the real collection process.
