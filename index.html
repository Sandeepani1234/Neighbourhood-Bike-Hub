Booking rules

  • One bike per booking.
  • Bookings cannot overlap for the same bike.
  • Return the bike on time and lock it securely.
  • Report damage before the next resident uses it.
  • The housing provider should set a late-return and damage policy before launch.
Suggested launch offer: the first five confirmed bookings per resident are free, regardless of bike type. After that, the normal rate applies. The housing provider can change this rule.

Proposed housing-provider standard

How the star system works

The stars rate how well a housing provider supports cycling at its residential property. They do not rate individual residents or the quality of a specific bicycle.

Important: This is our proposed concept for the project. Energicentrum Gotland has not confirmed these official levels or criteria.

★ Bronze

Basic cycling support

  • Convenient bicycle parking
  • Working pump and basic repair tools
  • Clear signs and QR instructions
  • Someone is responsible for maintenance
Starting level

★★ Silver

More convenient cycling

Must meet all Bronze criteria, plus:

  • Secure / weather-protected storage
  • Shared standard bicycles residents can book
  • Clear collection and return process
  • Regular checks are recorded
Bronze + extra services

★★★ Gold

A strong cycling offer

Must meet all Silver criteria, plus:

  • E-bike and/or cargo-bike access
  • Fault reporting and response process
  • Usage monitoring and resident feedback
  • Annual improvement plan
Silver + advanced services

Think of it like levels in a game

★ Bronze = Start

The basics are available.

★★ Silver = Improve

Residents can borrow bikes and store them securely.

★★★ Gold = Excellent support

The provider offers different bike types, tracks issues and improves the service.

A provider earns a level only when it meets the checklist for that level. The levels are cumulative: to earn Gold, it must also meet Bronze and Silver criteria.
